Packages that fit the way you run governance

Three packages: run it yourself, have a dedicated governance officer run it for you, or a full service. Your offer is set by your entity type.

Request a quote for your entity

Choose the package by who runs governance in your company. In every package each entity sees only the services that fit it.

Self (ذاتي)

You run it yourself

For teams that want to operate the platform in-house.

  • The core modules for your entity: meetings, decisions, policies and authorities
  • Regulatory compliance, performance evaluation and indicators dashboard
  • Electronic signature and mobile and email notifications
  • Operated and managed by your own team
Madar (مدار)

A dedicated governance officer

For those who want managed corporate governance without building a team.

  • Everything in Self
  • A dedicated governance officer from our team who manages your company's file
  • Follow-up of meetings, decisions and deadlines
  • Periodic reports for management and the board
Partner (شريك)

A full service

For those who want a partner covering governance, risk and audit.

  • Everything in Madar
  • Risk assessment and internal audit
  • A dedicated governance officer acting as relationship manager
  • Full coverage of the platform's services

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the three packages?

In Self (Dhati) you run the platform yourself; Madar adds a dedicated governance officer from our team who manages your file; Partner (Sharik) includes everything in Madar plus risk assessment and internal audit.

How much do the packages cost?

The offer depends on the entity type (sole proprietorship, limited liability company or joint-stock company), so we prepare a tailored quote once we know your entity. Contact us on WhatsApp.

Can I move from one package to another?

Yes, you can start with Self and move to Madar or Partner when you need to.

Is the offer for one company or several?

The offer is per entity, so if you have several companies each one is added as a separate subscription according to its entity type.

Is there a trial period?

Yes, there is a 14-day trial to explore the platform before subscribing. It is for exploration and does not include delivery of services.

Which package fits your entity?

Tell us your entity type and we will prepare a suitable offer.
