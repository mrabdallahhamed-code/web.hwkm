Overview

Disclosure of material information requires listed companies to issue accurate, timely announcements through Tadawul. Drafting each announcement from scratch takes time and effort, and any drafting error or delay in issuing it may carry a regulatory consequence.

That is why legal and investor relations teams need ready templates, clear review and approval procedures, and an archive that proves what was announced and when.

How HWKM helps

86 ready-drafted, electronically signed announcement types, prepared for submission to Tadawul in minutes — not hours with legal counsel drafting every announcement from scratch.

HWKM provides 86 ready-drafted announcement types, with review and preparation of the announcement before it is submitted to Tadawul, electronic signature on the document before approval, and archiving and status tracking for every announcement.

Result: disclosures for listed companies (Tadawul announcements) submitted accurately and fast — no drafting risk, no delay.
