Security and Data Protection

Governance data is sensitive: board decisions, policies and minutes. Here we state plainly what we actually apply to protect it, and what we do not claim.

Each company's data is isolated

Every company's data is isolated from other companies at the database level, so a user of one company cannot see another company's data. We ran an internal isolation test to verify this.

Who gets in and what they see

  • Sign-in requires email confirmation and account activation by your company's system administrator.
  • Each user has permissions based on their role (system administrator, board secretary, board member and others) and sees only what concerns them.
  • Sign-in is protected against automated attempts.
  • Two-step verification is available for accounts, and we will make it mandatory for everyone once paid subscriptions begin.

Activity log and documentation

Important operations (create, edit, approve, sign) are recorded in an activity log showing who did what and when. Electronic signatures and approvals are stored with their history.

Protecting and preserving data

  • Connections to the platform and website are encrypted over HTTPS.
  • We keep periodic encrypted backups stored separately from the primary database.
  • We monitor platform availability and technical errors so we can respond quickly to any outage.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are optional and run only when you request them. Their outputs are suggestions the user reviews before adopting, and the whole platform can be used without them.

Data ownership

Your company's data belongs to it. To ask about exporting your data or ending your subscription, contact us.

What we do not claim

No system is absolutely secure, and we do not offer such a guarantee. We have not yet obtained a formal security certification such as ISO 27001, and we keep improving our controls. The platform is a tool for organizing and documenting governance and does not replace your legal advisor.

Security questions or a questionnaire for your company?

If your IT or compliance team has questions or a security questionnaire, contact us at info@hwkm.net and we will answer what applies to your case. For how we handle website data, see the Privacy Policy Security.
