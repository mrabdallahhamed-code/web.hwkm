About the platform

What is HWKM?

HWKM is a Saudi platform with Arabic and English interfaces that brings corporate governance, risk and compliance into one system: it manages board meetings, general assemblies, internal audit, risk management and compliance, and prepares disclosures for listed companies (Tadawul announcements). It is owned and operated by Asas Alanzima Aljadida in Riyadh.

Optional AI features

The platform suggests job descriptions, improves the wording of procedures, and analyses an operational-results file to surface variances. These features are optional, run only when requested, and the user reviews their output before approving it.

Data isolation and security for every company

Each company's data is isolated from every other company at the database level. Sign-in requires email confirmation and account activation by the system administrator, with protection against automated sign-in and an activity log that records operations. See the privacy policy for details.
