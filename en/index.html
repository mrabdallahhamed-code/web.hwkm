About Us

Our Story Started Inside Companies

HWKM was not born from theory or academic study — it was born from the field. For over a decade, our team sat with boards of directors and executive teams in the Saudi market, witnessing firsthand the real challenges companies face in implementing governance.

We saw carefully written policies that were never applied. We witnessed critical decisions made verbally without documentation. We observed how time and effort were wasted managing meetings and documents through email and Excel files.

From this field reality, and from the frustration of the absence of an integrated Arabic solution, the idea of HWKM was born — a platform that takes the company by the hand from initial assessment to the actual implementation of governance on the ground.

FAQ

Answers to the Most Common Questions

If you can't find an answer to your question, don't hesitate to contact us directly.

What makes HWKM different?▼
HWKM is an integrated governance, risk and compliance system that brings seven solutions into one platform: corporate governance, board meetings, general assemblies, internal audit, risk management, compliance, and disclosures for listed companies (Tadawul announcements). It is designed for the Saudi market with an Arabic interface, and includes electronic signature, automatic archiving, and 86 ready-drafted announcement types.
Is HWKM aligned with the Corporate Governance Regulations? ▼
The platform is designed according to the Corporate Governance Regulations issued by the Saudi Capital Market Authority (CMA) and the requirements of the Ministry of Commerce, and we track regulatory updates and update the platform accordingly.
Is HWKM suitable for small companies?▼
Yes. HWKM is designed to serve companies of all sizes, from startups seeking institutionalization to large and listed companies. We have flexible solutions that suit every stage of company growth.
How long does it take to implement the platform in our company?▼
The time varies depending on company size, current status, and requirements. Implementation includes the assessment phase, building the governance structure, team training, and actual operation.
Do you provide support after implementation?▼
Absolutely. We believe in long-term partnership. We provide ongoing support including regulatory updates, training for new members, technical assistance, and periodic reviews to ensure governance effectiveness.
