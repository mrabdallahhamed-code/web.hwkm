Who we serve

The needs of a listed joint-stock company differ from those of a sole proprietorship, so HWKM shows each entity the services and pages that fit it, with permissions defining what each user can see and edit. Here is a quick look at what we offer each entity.

Listed joint-stock companies

  • Corporate governance, board and committee structure
  • Board and committee meetings and minutes
  • Disclosures for Listed Companies (Tadawul Announcements): 86 ready-drafted announcement types
  • Compliance and electronic signature
  • Internal audit and risk management (add-on modules)

Unlisted joint-stock companies

  • Corporate governance, board and committee structure
  • Board and committee meetings and minutes
  • General assemblies and electronic voting
  • Compliance and electronic signature
  • Internal audit and risk management (add-on modules)

Limited liability companies

  • Whether your limited liability company has a single owner or several partners, HWKM provides services according to the ownership type.
  • Single owner: governance, the owner's decisions register, decision follow-up, electronic signature, audit and risk, and compliance.
  • Several partners: everything above, plus electronic voting and managers' board and partners' meetings.

Sole proprietorships

  • Organizing internal governance
  • Owner's decisions register
  • Electronic signature for decisions
  • Risk follow-up and audit (add-on modules)
  • Compliance

The services shown vary by entity type and are set when the account is created.

Frequently asked questions

Does HWKM suit every entity type?

Yes. HWKM is designed to suit listed and unlisted joint-stock companies, limited liability companies and sole proprietorships, and each entity sees the services that fit it.

Are disclosures for listed companies available to every entity?

No. Disclosures for Listed Companies (Tadawul Announcements) are for listed companies.

Yes, a 14-day trial period is available when you create an account.
