Overview

Regulatory compliance is a company's adherence to the laws and regulations governing its activity, foremost the Corporate Governance Regulations and the requirements of the Capital Market Authority and the Ministry of Commerce, depending on the company's entity. Its difficulty lies in the number of obligations, their many deadlines, and requirements that change from time to time.

When these obligations are managed in scattered sheets, it is hard to know what has been met, what has been missed and what is coming due, and it is hard to provide evidence on request from a regulator or during an audit.

How HWKM helps

Clearer compliance. Alerts before deadlines, an up-to-date obligations register, and ready reports — instead of discovering a missed obligation when it is too late.

HWKM keeps an up-to-date register of regulatory obligations, sends alerts about deadlines and requirements before they fall due, follows compliance with the Capital Market Authority, and issues periodic compliance reports that help you prepare for any audit or regulatory review.

Result: An organized compliance record that eases preparation for any audit or regulatory review.
