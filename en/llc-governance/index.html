Governance for Limited Liability Companies

Managers and partners meetings, decisions and signatures — in one organized, documented place, instead of scattered messages and files.

Start Your Free Trial
Overview

Many limited liability companies are run between managers and partners over WhatsApp, email and scattered files. Decisions get lost and it becomes hard to prove who approved what, and when. These companies do not necessarily need everything a listed joint-stock company needs — they need simple, documented organization that fits their size.

HWKM gives limited liability companies corporate governance, management of managers' board and partners' meetings, decision follow-up, e-signatures, internal audit and risk management, with permissions that define what each person can see and edit. Pages specific to joint-stock companies (such as Tadawul announcements) are not relevant to this entity type, so they are not shown.

The challenge companies face

Partner and manager decisions are made on calls and in messages, then forgotten. Minutes are written late or not at all, and it is hard to go back and see who agreed to what. When a new partner joins or a lender asks for documents, collecting the records is hard work.

How HWKM helps

HWKM organizes meetings and agendas, records the vote on each decision, and produces the minutes and sends them for e-signature. Everything is kept in one record you can return to.

Permissions are role-based: only the system administrator edits data, other users see only what concerns them, and the administrator can grant edit access to a specific person when needed.

Key features

  • Corporate governance and company structure
  • Managers' board and partners' meetings and agendas
  • Documented electronic voting on every decision
  • Minutes generated and sent for e-signature
  • Decision follow-up and a register you can refer back to
  • Internal audit and risk management (add-on modules)
